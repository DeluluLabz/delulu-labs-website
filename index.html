Game 01

cringe!

A party game for the nights when nobody wants to answer first. Four games in one app. Pick one, pass the phone around, and try to keep your dignity.

  • 😬Cringe questionsAnswer honestly or take the penalty.
  • 💸How much would you take?Guess the price your friend would do a dare for. Closest guess wins.

Game 02

Masks OffALSO KNOWN AS راوی

The host's sidekick for hidden-role games. Everyone wears a mask until the last vote, when the masks come off and you find out who was lying. Masks Off keeps the roles, the turns and the votes, so the host can enjoy the game too. In Persian it's called Ravi, the narrator.

With a narrator

The phone stays with the host and walks them through every night and day.

  • 🔫MafiaGodfather, doctor, detective, sniper. Find the mafia before they find you.
  • 🐺WerewolfWolves at night, villagers by day. The seer and the guard try to keep the town alive.

Quick games, no narrator

Pass the phone around and start in a minute.

  • 🕵️SpyEveryone knows the place. Except one.
  • 👑King, Thief & MinisterSecret roles. Loud accusations.

How Masks Off helps the host

Deals the rolesEach player looks at the phone alone and sees only their own role.
Runs night and dayLines to read out loud, night choices, talk timers and votes, step by step.
Calls the winnerMasks Off keeps score and tells you who won. No arguing over notes.
